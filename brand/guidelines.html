01 — BRAND FOUNDATION

Titles — the credibility rule

Titles lead with what is provable: Founder and Advisor. Softer, conferred titles — mentor, educator, expert, guru — are demonstrated through the work and never written into his own bio. Tenure is always “16 years in the creative industry”, never “16 years as a creative director”. Photography and creative direction are the source of authority, not a service for sale. The Fujifilm ambassadorship is quiet background credibility — present, never leading.

Public bio — say
  • Creative AI Founder & Advisor
  • Founder of MA Learn & MA Studio
  • 16 years in the creative industry
  • 3× founder · trusted by companies locally and globally
Public bio — never
  • “The bridge between creativity and AI” — internal strategy shorthand only; self-declaring it reads grandiose.
  • Mentor · educator · expert · guru as self-labels
  • Working photographer / creative director for hire
  • “MA Group” — does not exist yet

The two identities (content lock)

Every week of content must show both: 1) the best creative educator who helps creatives build a business, and 2) the AI creative-director figure in the fashion industry — work, not teaching. Neither alone is enough. Every piece must activate at least one of Person (face, voice, story), Saudi / Arabic (dialect, cultural reference), or Lineage (16 years, real campaigns, eye-before-tool).

What we refuse to be

Simple photographer · simple freelancer / service provider · employee · priced cheaply · hustle-bro flexing wealth · “I'm just like you” relatability theater. The relationship target is the humble, successful person who stays close to the people — visible success, visible humility, visible care.

Who we speak to — المبدع الباني

The Creative Builder: a Saudi/GCC creative, 28–38, already working professionally, earning 8–25K SAR a month and aspiring to 30–100K, premium-conscious, anti-bargain. Lighthouse: a 32-year-old fashion photographer in Jeddah or Riyadh who just lost a pitch to a younger AI-savvy creator and is considering opening an agency. Three pains stack: talented but stuck, AI anxiety, no leverage. The transformation: AI as leverage for a business and life they're proud of.

03 — THE MARK

The MA lettermark

Three grouped angular strokes form the letters M and A at once: the outer groups are the legs of the M; the centre stroke is the valley of the M and the peak of the A. Sharp, architectural, precise — creative vision with structural discipline.

ElementMA LearnMajid Angawi
Mark weightLight / refinedBold / authoritative
WordmarkLEARN — lightMAJID ANGAWI — bold, stacked
Primary useWebsite, player, checkout, coursesLink in bio, socials, personal work, decks
Fileslogo-malearn-white.png · logo-malearn-black.pnglogo-majid-white.png · logo-majid-black.png

Rules

  • The mark is strictly monochrome: cream/white on dark surfaces, black on light. Never orange, never lavender, never a gradient.
  • Clear space all round equal to the height of the letter A in the wordmark. Minimum width 80px digital, 25mm print.
  • Never stretch, rotate, outline, add shadow or glow, or place on a busy image without a scrim.
  • Never place the MA Learn and Majid Angawi logos side by side without intentional design context (a co-branded deck cover, for example).
  • In a V4 header the logo sits at 30–34px height on the scrim bar; on a hero it may be replaced by the wordmark treatment “MA | LEARN” in accent only when the image logo cannot load.

04 — COLOR

A poster in a dark room

V4 retires the near-black + heritage-gold palette. The ground is now a warm poster brown, the ink is cream, and the accent is a single inspiration orange — the colour of the one warm light source the old guideline described, finally made literal. Lavender is the calm counterpoint for foundations and community.

How the palette is used

  • 70 / 20 / 10. Surfaces ~70% of any view, ink and lines ~20%, accent ~10%. Orange is loud by design, so it is rare.
  • Orange owns the CTA. One primary button per view. The highlighted word in a heading (.hl) is the only other place orange text appears at size.
  • Lavender cools a section. Included value (ITCAI), community, foundations. A whole card or section goes lavender — never a lavender button beside an orange one.
  • Lines are cream at 11% / 20%. Never solid grey borders.
  • Semantic colours (green / amber / red) are muted and used only for state: live, started, urgent, sold out, error.
  • Retired: heritage gold #C9A84C, luminous gold #E8D08A, void black #080808. The only surviving gold is the player's loading mark, pinned by Majid.
Do
  • Tint with alpha versions of the accent for fills and borders.
  • Put the hero glow at the top of a page, once.
  • Keep photos warm-graded so they sit on the brown ground.
Don't
  • Introduce a new hue — no blue, teal, pink, neon green.
  • Use pure black or pure white anywhere.
  • Gradient text, glowing text, orange on lavender.

Language rules in type

  • Arabic is primary. Brand and product names stay in Latin — MA Learn, Midjourney, Higgsfield, AI — never transliterated.
  • Arabic-Indic numerals (٢٥–٢٧ أغسطس) for dates and counts inside Arabic copy; Latin digits for prices (1,399 SAR), statistics (16 · 500+) and anything tabular.
  • No all-caps Arabic. Latin uppercase only for short labels (DESIGN SYSTEM, LEARN) with wide tracking.
  • Majid's Arabic name is spelled ماجد عنقاوي. The word for cohort is دفعة — never كوهورت.
06 — LAYOUT & SHAPE

Centered, airy, chunky

  • Centered by default. Headings, leads, chips and CTAs align to the centre of a 1120px column. Left/right alignment is reserved for lists, forms and app shells.
  • Sections breathe. 88–176px vertical rhythm on marketing pages; 56–108px on dense pages and app UI. The eye rests between ideas.
  • Chunky corners. 22px on cards, 28px on feature cards, 34px on hero panels, pills on everything you can press. Nothing interactive is squarer than 10px.
  • 1.5px lines. Every border is a cream line at 11% (default) or 20% (interactive). Hairlines (1px) only for list rows and the footer.
  • Light from the top. One warm glow on the hero. Cards float on a line, not a shadow; only the primary button casts orange light.
  • One column tells the story. Hero → clarification → proof → offer → curriculum → about → FAQ → CTA. Grids of two or three appear only for peers (cards, tiles, testimonials).
  • Mobile is the first read. Design at 390px, then let it grow. Sticky CTA bar appears after the hero scrolls out.
07 — VOICE & LANGUAGE

A friend and mentor, not an instructor

Warm, calm, wise. Direct because we care. Light humour is welcome; tough love is encouraged; condescension and corporate tone never. The person who doesn't need to raise his voice because what he says carries weight.

RegisterWhereHow
Arabic — Saudi / Hijazi dialectAll customer-facing copy: pages, emails, DMs, community, videoWarmer and more direct than English. Never formal فصحى for marketing. Inspirational, blunt, funny.
EnglishTechnical vocabulary, LinkedIn, partners, internalSame warmth, slightly tighter. Friend and mentor.
First person, argumentativePaid statics, hooks, hot takesSpeak as a person making a deal; voice the objection then overturn it; end on a question; use a specific number.

Hard rules (every writer, every agent)

Always
  • Plain value exchange: what you get, what it costs, when. Compassion over pressure.
  • Real numbers only — pulled, never projected.
  • “بث مباشر غير مسجل” is the only scarcity we state, because it is true.
  • Bundle components are “included” (مضمّن), never “gifts”.
  • Testimonials or a student result in every launch piece.
  • Close with ألهم where a stamp fits.
Never
  • False scarcity — no “ما ينعاد”. We repeat lives.
  • Guru framing — no “access to Majid”, no “secrets”.
  • Exaggeration, hype, superlatives without proof.
  • Internal acronyms in public (CIW, ITCAI, T3, C4). Say the product's name.
  • Emojis in emails; unsolicited emojis anywhere.
  • Announcing the “sister voice” or the “bridge” — internal framings stay internal.

Naming

  • Products carry their Arabic names publicly: معادلة صناعة الإلهام 2.0 · مدخل إلى الذكاء الاصطناعي الإبداعي · أبعد من إمكانيات الإضاءة · مجتمع صُنّاع الإلهام (full name, no acronym).
  • Cohorts are numbered in Arabic: الدفعة الرابعة, never “C4” outside the team.
08 — IMAGERY & MOTION

Reference over invention

Our images look photographed, not rendered. They carry the lineage — 16 years of fashion and jewellery campaigns — into AI work. The visual formula book is co-built by Majid with Layan (review) and Nahar (production).

  • Name the legend. Every visual brief cites a real reference — a photographer, a creative director, a cinematographer — never “make it cinematic”.
  • Warm grade. Images sit on the brown ground: warm shadows, cream highlights, restrained saturation. Orange appears in the image only when the subject brings it.
  • Real people, real work. Majid's face and voice, student results, screenshots of actual sessions. No stock, no generic AI faces. No named celebrities in prompts.
  • Soul-id / likeness models are R&D only — never a shipped output, including Majid's own.
  • Layan reviews, Majid signs. Nothing visual ships without both — five axes, minimum 7/10 each: craft, reference fidelity, brand fit, cultural fit, detail integrity.
  • Media frames use the 22px slot with a cream line; captions are pills on a scrim; empty slots are dashed placeholders, never blank.

Motion

One easing curve — cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) — for everything. Hover lifts 2px, cards 4px. Sections reveal once as they enter (26px, 800ms). Marquees loop seamlessly. Pulses only for live urgency. No bounce, no parallax, no animated gradients. Reduced-motion collapses everything; the page still works.

Loaders. The “جاري صناعة الإلهام” Gumela loader is the one exception to the retired gold — Majid's call. It stays as it is.

10 — ACCESSIBILITY & RTL

Readable by everyone, native in Arabic

  • Ink on ground is 16:1; body muted is 10.5:1; labels 5.6:1; accent-hot on ground 7.6:1; on-accent on accent 6.9:1. Never place readable copy in --faint-2.
  • Focus is visible: a 2px orange ring at 35%. Every interactive element is reachable by keyboard.
  • Reduced motion is honoured through the tokens; reveals resolve to visible.
  • Minimum tap target 44px; minimum body 16px on mobile; line-height 1.75+ for Arabic.
  • RTL-first: logical CSS properties only. Marquee and media tracks are internally LTR. Numbers and Latin fragments carry dir="ltr" where they would otherwise mirror.
  • Alt text on every meaningful image, in the page's language.
