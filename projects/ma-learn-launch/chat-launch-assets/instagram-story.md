# Instagram Story — Chat Launch

**Goal:** quick reveal + urgency to check it out. 1 frame, stays up 24h.

## Visual direction

- Dark background (Editorial Atelier `--c-ink-0` = near-black warm)
- Screenshot of the player with Discussion tab active, showing 2-3 messages (use your own test messages, blur student names)
- Gold hairline underneath the tab (the new primitive)
- Overlay text at top: "جديد داخل المنصة" in Cairo 700 white
- Overlay at bottom: "افتح درسك واكتب سؤالك" in Cairo 600 gold
- "Swipe up" sticker (or link sticker) → `player.malearnsa.com/watch.html?token=...&course=beyond-lighting`

## Copy options (pick one)

**Pick A for launch day.** B and C are follow-up stories for day 2 and day 3.
