## Design notes

- Every slide uses the Editorial Atelier palette: `--c-ink-0` bg, `--c-fg` text, `--c-gold` accents.
- Cairo 200 for display quotes (slide 1), Cairo 700 for headlines.
- 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait) — max mobile real estate.
- Export via Canva or Figma. Don't use stock templates — the brand voice is editorial, not "SaaS product announcement."
- Optional: each slide has a small slide-number indicator (`1 / 6`) in the top-right in Cairo mono style.

## When to post

3–5 days after the Reel. Carousel is the "educational keeper" post — it's what people save and share.
